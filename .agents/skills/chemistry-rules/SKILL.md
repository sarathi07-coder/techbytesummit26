# Chemistry Domain Knowledge & Degradation Rules

## 1. Peroxide-Forming Chemical Degradation Timeline
Peroxide formers react with atmospheric oxygen through light-activated auto-oxidation to form shock-sensitive, explosive organic peroxides.

### Class A (Severe Peroxide Hazard - Test every 3 months or discard):
- Diisopropyl ether (DIPE)
- Divinyl acetylene
- Potassium metal
- Sodium amide

### Class B (Concentration Hazard - Maximum 6 months after opening):
- Diethyl ether (CAS: 60-29-7): Critical post-opening limit is 180 days. Presence of crystals around cap neck indicates high shock-detonation risk.
- Tetrahydrofuran (THF, CAS: 109-99-9): Max 180 days post-opening.
- 2-Propanol / Isopropanol (CAS: 67-63-0): Max 365 days.
- 1,4-Dioxane (CAS: 123-91-1): Max 180 days.

## 2. Hygroscopic Reagents (Monsoon & Power Cut Vulnerability)
- Sodium Hydroxide ($NaOH$, CAS: 1310-73-2): Deliquescent. Absorbs $CO_2$ and $H_2O$ from air. Forms solid crust.
- Calcium Chloride ($CaCl_2$, CAS: 10043-52-4): Rapid liquefaction in $>70\%$ humidity.

## 3. Photolytic (Light-Sensitive) Degradation
- Silver Nitrate ($AgNO_3$, CAS: 7761-88-8): Decomposes to metallic silver under ambient light exposure if amber bottle is cracked or left unshaded.

## 4. GHS Storage Incompatibility Matrix
Never store in the same cabinet or shelf:
1. **Inorganic Mineral Acids** (e.g. $HCl, H_2SO_4, HNO_3$) + **Organic Solvents / Flammables** (Acetone, Ethanol, Diethyl ether).
2. **Nitric Acid ($HNO_3$)** + **Organic Acids** (Glacial Acetic Acid) $\to$ Violent exothermic oxidation and gas generation.
3. **Concentrated Acids** + **Concentrated Bases** ($NaOH, KOH$) $\to$ Neutralization explosion risk.
4. **Strong Oxidizers** ($KMnO_4, H_2O_2$) + **Reducing Agents / Flammables** $\to$ Spontaneous ignition risk.
